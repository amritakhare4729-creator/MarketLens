Indicator Guide

What the main technical indicators represent.

RSI

RSI measures recent price momentum. Lower readings can indicate weaker momentum, while higher readings indicate stronger momentum.

Moving Averages

Moving averages smooth historical prices. Comparing the closing price with the 20-day and 50-day averages helps identify the current trend.

MACD

MACD compares short-term and long-term price momentum. The MACD and signal line are used together to understand momentum changes.
